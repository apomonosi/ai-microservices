Equipment Booking (fictional example content, department knowledge base)

The department maintains a small pool of shared equipment: two portable
projectors, a 360-degree conference camera, and a set of voice recorders
for fieldwork. All are stored in the equipment cupboard in Room 214.

To book an item, email the department administrator with the item, the
dates needed, and your project or course code. Bookings are confirmed on
a first-come, first-served basis and are not guaranteed until you
receive a confirmation email.

Equipment must be returned within one working day of the booked end
date. Persistent late returns may result in booking priority being given
to other staff and students for future requests.

Damaged or faulty equipment should be reported immediately to the
department administrator, not left in the cupboard for the next person
to discover.
