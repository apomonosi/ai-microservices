This is a fictional example department FAQ used to demonstrate the
department-knowledge-assistant service. Names, rooms and procedures
below are invented for demonstration only.

How do I get a key to my office? Contact the department administrator,
Room 210, with your staff ID. Keys are usually issued within two working
days.

How do I book a meeting room? Meeting rooms are booked through the
shared departmental calendar. Ask the department administrator for
calendar access if you don't already have it.

Who do I contact about a broken printer? Report printer problems to IT
Support via the internal helpdesk portal, not the department office.

How do I claim travel expenses for a conference? Submit receipts and a
completed expense form to the department administrator within one month
of travel. Reimbursement is normally processed within four weeks.

Where can new starters find the department handbook? The department
handbook is available on the internal staff wiki, under Department
Resources. It covers onboarding, expectations for new staff, and key
contacts.

Who approves new PhD student admissions? The department's graduate
studies committee reviews and approves all PhD admissions, meeting
monthly during the admissions cycle.
