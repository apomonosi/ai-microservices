Late Submission Policy for Coursework

Coursework submitted after its published deadline is considered late.
Students are expected to submit work on time except in cases covered by
this policy.

A student may submit coursework up to 24 hours late without penalty if
they notify the course instructor before the original deadline. This
applies at most twice per course per term.

Beyond the 24-hour grace period, or without prior notification, late
coursework is penalized at five percent of the total available marks per
day late, including weekends, up to a maximum of five days.

Coursework submitted more than five days late will not be marked unless
the student has an approved extenuating-circumstances extension on file
with the department office.

Extenuating circumstances (documented illness, bereavement, or similar)
should be reported to the department office as soon as practical, and
normally within five working days of the affected deadline. The
department office, not the individual instructor, decides whether an
extension is granted.

This policy applies to individual coursework. Group coursework deadlines
and extensions are set by the module convener at the start of term and
may differ from this policy.
